CLID: CROSS-LINGUAL INSTRUCTION DISTILLATION FOR LOW-RESOURCE EXPRESSIVE EMOTIONAL TEXT-TO-SPEECH

Code; Paper (coming soon)

Abstract Despite rapid progress, instruction-guided text-to-speech (TTS) systems still rely on paired expressive speech-instruction datasets for emotional speech generation. To alleviate this burden, we introduce a cross-lingual instruction distillation (CLID) method, which transfers the instruction-following capability of a pretrained TTS model across instruction language. Using semantically aligned English-Korean instructions with the same spoken text, CLID distills the teacher's speech-token distributions into a student while updating. We further apply CFG to construct guided teacher targets and distill them into a Korean-SFT student, yielding CLID-TTS with improved emotion controllability and speech quality. The experimental results demonstrated that the CLID improves Korean instruction following without Korean Instruction-speech pair data, while CLID-TTS further improves emotion accuracy and speech intelligibility. Audio samples are available at https://speechai-lab.github.io/demo/clid-tts/.
