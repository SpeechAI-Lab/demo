Streaming · Korean news clips

Demo videos

MEARI translates each clip frame by frame as it streams. Stereo: left channel is MEARI's English speech, right channel is the original Korean (7 dB lower). Headphones recommended.

Each clip was decoded with three random seeds; the take with the highest reference-free CometKiwi score (Korean ASR transcript vs. English ASR transcript of MEARI's speech) is shown. Scores for whole 37–56 s passages are rough. Clips are ordered by that score, including the weaker sports clips. Source videos belong to their broadcasters and are used here for research demonstration only.
